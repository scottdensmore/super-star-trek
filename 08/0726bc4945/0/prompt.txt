## One nit — and explicitly *not* the shape you are hunting

**`tests/tui.sh:987` — `'^x 10 '` is the only assertion in the suite that depends on how `capture-pane` renders the line-drawing set.** The file's own `uniform_col` comment establishes the opposite convention: "Which character it is does not matter — capture-pane renders the line-drawing set as it pleases." The `x` is ACS_VLINE's ASCII fallback.

It is safe today for a reason I checked rather than assumed: nothing in the tree calls `setlocale()`, so ncurses runs in the C locale and emits ACS escapes, which tmux stores and replays as `x`/`q` — on macOS by the same reasoning. **And its failure mode is a spurious FAIL, never a silent pass**, so it cannot hide a defect. That is the opposite of the three assertions this branch has already produced, and I do not want the framing of your question to push it up a severity it does not deserve.

If you want it render-agnostic anyway, `'^. 10  '` is a verified drop-in — I ran all three candidates against a real 80x24 capture:

| pattern | lines matched |
|---|---|
| `^x 10 ` | 1 (grid row 10) |
| `^. 10  ` | 1 (grid row 10) |
| ` 10  ` | **3** — header, `Torpedoes     10`, grid row 10 |

The third is the trap: dropping the anchor to escape the `x` would silently weaken the check to something the column header satisfies on its own. `^. 10  ` keeps the selectivity because the header has one space after its leading `1` where a grid row has two — the same spacing fact the two-labels regex now rests on. Your call; leaving it is defensible, and I would not hold the commit for it.
