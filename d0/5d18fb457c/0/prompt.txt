### 2. `start_shell()` truncates the developer's `~/.bash_history` — deterministic

`--norc --noprofile` does not turn history off. The pane's interactive bash loads `$HISTFILE` (default `~/.bash_history`) at startup and writes it back when it exits on the hangup from `cleanup`'s `kill-server`. With no rc file, `histappend` is off and `HISTFILESIZE` defaults to 500, so the file is **rewritten truncated to the last 500 lines**.

Measured through the real script, with a throwaway HOME holding 700 lines:
