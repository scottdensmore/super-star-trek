### 2. The comment's budget arithmetic is wrong, in a comment AGENTS.md reads as specification

`ci.yml:50-51`: "three tries at two minutes cost at most six of the fifteen rather than all of them."

A try is two bounded commands, not one: `timeout 120` plus `timeout 180` is 300s. The `sleep 10` also runs after the third failed attempt, since it is inside the loop body. - **The three-attempt design is not reachable in the worst case.** If `apt-get install` is the hanging command rather than `apt-get update`, an attempt costs `t_update + 180 + 10` ≈ 210s, so attempt 3 starts at ~420s and is cut off, or never starts at all if attempts run to their full 300s (attempt 3 would begin at 620s &gt; 480s).
