**`help &lt;command&gt;` displays nothing but blank lines in full-screen mode.** I ran `help move` in the TUI at both 80x24 and 100x30 and got `Spock- "Captain, I've found the following information:"` followed by page after page of empty lines and `[HIT SPACE BAR TO CONTINUE]`. The cause: `sst.doc` has CRLF line endings (`file` reports "ASCII text, with CRLF line terminators"), and `helpme()` strips only the `\n`, leaving a trailing `\r` on every line. I confirmed the mechanism with a minimal curses probe:

```c
waddstr(w, "LINE-A plain");    waddstr(w, "\n");
waddstr(w, "LINE-B with CR\r"); waddstr(w, "\n");
waddstr(w, "LINE-C plain");    waddstr(w, "\n");
```
renders as `LINE-A plain`, a blank line, `LINE-C plain`.

Worth its own issue. The fix is one line in `helpme()` (strip a trailing `\r` as well as `\n`), or more defensively in `tui_puts()`. It also means any future output line ending in `\r` will silently vanish in TUI mode, which is worth a note in AGENTS.md alongside the existing "all output through `prout`" rule.
