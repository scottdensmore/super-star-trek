**Can the canary go wrong?** Two ways, both fail-closed, one worth a note.

*Robust to* — I could not break it with `$HOME/.shellcheckrc` containing `disable=SC2086` or `severity=error`, nor with a `.shellcheckrc` in the cwd; actionlint 1.7.12 apparently does not let shellcheck read rc files. CI pins 1.7.12 so CI is insulated from version drift; a developer on a newer actionlint whose output changed would silently lose the check to a SKIP, which is the safe direction.
